CSV format

One row per product per shop. Columns (header row required):

  • date — YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY
  • product_name, quantity
  • unit_price or line_total
  • Optional: basket_id, channel, cup_price (e.g. $1.51 / 100G), stockcode

A PDF invoice goes to a review page first: check every parsed line against the subtotal, fix mistakes, then confirm. CSV files import immediately with no review. Re-importing the same invoice or file is safe: lines are flagged as already imported and nothing is duplicated. Unavailable items (not supplied) and $0.00 freebies are skipped.
