How suggestions work
  • Bank transactions: every imported or synced transaction is stored. A merchant with three or more payments on a regular cadence becomes a suggestion (two is enough for a yearly bill). Fixed amounts are marked as subscriptions; variable amounts (utilities) as bills.
  • Merchant names: known Australian brands (Netflix, Telstra, AGL, insurers) are named and categorised automatically. Everything else uses the description on the transaction.
  • Purchase history: regularly bought groceries appear separately below as "Often bought — not bills". They are never added to your subscriptions.
  • Suggestions are opt-in: nothing is added until you click Add. Dismiss hides a merchant for good, and dismissed merchants can be restored below.
