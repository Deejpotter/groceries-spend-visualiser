How this is worked out
  • Pay is your regular salary credit. Costs are bank spending from {{ advice.start|nice_date }} to {{ advice.end|nice_date }}, scaled to one pay.
  • Transfers between your own accounts are moves, so they are not counted as costs or income.
  • A fifth of what is left ({{ advice.buffer|money }}) stays as a buffer for irregular costs.
  • The rest is split in half. Long term savings build an emergency fund of three months of bills and groceries.
  • Saving 20% of your pay is a common benchmark, not a rule.
