Capture prices from your browser

All prices

The stores' websites sometimes block this app, and Coles currently does. A bookmark in your own browser reads the price from the page you're looking at, so it works whenever the store's website works for you. It also reconnects the daily Coles check.

Set it up once
  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome and Edge).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar: Capture prices
  3. You should see Capture prices on the bar.
Can't drag it? (phones and some browsers)

Bookmark any page, edit the bookmark, and paste this as its address:

Use it
  1. Open a product page, a search or a category on Coles, Woolworths or Aldi.
  2. Click Capture prices on your bookmarks bar.
  3. A tab opens here with the products it found. Pick the ingredient for any you want to track and choose Save prices. Products you already track are saved without asking.

Woolworths search pages don't hold their prices in the page, so use a Woolworths product page. Nothing is read until you click the bookmark.
