Stock count

Pantry Shopping list

Count what's left before you order, in packs (a 1 L milk is 1, a 6-pack of rolls is 1). Leave a box blank to skip it. Each count teaches the app how fast you use things, and the pay-cycle list then buys only what runs short. Keep at most caps what fits in the cupboard; keeps for is for food that goes off, which is bought for that many days and topped up later.

Try counting from photos experiment

Take up to 4 photos of the cupboard, fridge and freezer with the labels facing out, and upload them together. The photos are sent to the AI model in Settings to guess the counts, and aren't stored. Each guess shows beside its count box. Still count by hand: the page compares the two so you can see whether photo counts are good enough to trust.
